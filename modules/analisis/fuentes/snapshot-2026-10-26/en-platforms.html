Platforms

From real-world challenges to platforms
that keep evolving.

At VORTEX we don't only build solutions for our clients. We also build products of our own, starting from specific needs we identify across industries.

We know the context, we understand the processes, and we turn those challenges into platforms that grow and evolve alongside the business.

QuickPass

From the challenge of running an event to a platform for the entire experience.

Events and live entertainment need far more than ticket sales. The operation brings together admission, ticketing, payments, spending, users and experience, and all of it has to work as one.

That is where QUICKPASS came from: a platform designed specifically for the live entertainment and events industry.

What it does

  • Digital ticketing
  • Access control
  • Wallet & cashless
  • Loyalty
  • Analytics
  • User management

A platform that evolves with the industry QUICKPASS keeps adding capabilities as the needs of events, their operators and their audiences change.

Loyalix

From loyalty programs to smarter customer relationships.

Brands need to know their customers better, tailor their rewards, and turn every interaction into a chance to strengthen the relationship.

That challenge led to LOYALIX, a loyalty platform for designing personalized experiences and running rewards programs from a single place.

What it does

  • Points programs
  • Coupons and promotions
  • Personalization
  • Segmentation
  • Analytics
  • Integration with existing platforms

A platform that adapts to each brand LOYALIX can plug into an existing app or ecosystem through APIs, or be deployed as a bespoke solution under the brand's own identity.

Omnia Talents

From reviewing resumes to running smarter hiring processes with AI.

HR teams need to analyze profiles, verify candidates and keep every stage of the process moving. As applications pile up, reviewing each profile takes more and more time and coordination.

That challenge led to OmnIA Talents, a platform that brings artificial intelligence into recruiting, candidate verification and professional development.

What it does

  • Resume analysis
  • Candidate screening
  • Asynchronous AI interviews
  • Identity verification
  • Profile-based recommendations
  • Professional development

A platform that strengthens talent management OmnIA Talents speeds up the evaluation stages and organizes the information that matters, to support both hiring and people's development.

Rutex AI

From using AI models to managing how they are used, all in one place.

As companies bring in artificial intelligence, they need to manage the models they use, keep costs in check, and maintain the quality, security and traceability of every interaction.

That challenge led to RUTEX AI, an enterprise AI platform that centralizes how model usage is managed and makes it easier to control across the organization.

What it does

  • Centralized model management
  • Cost control
  • Performance monitoring
  • Quality management
  • Security
  • Interaction traceability

A platform for managing enterprise AI RUTEX AI centralizes the administration of artificial intelligence usage and improves visibility and control as adoption spreads across the company.
