How we work

Transformation is not just about deploying technology.

It means understanding the business, defining what needs to change, designing the right strategy
and building solutions ready to evolve.

At VORTEX we combine business insight, technology and artificial intelligence
to support every stage of that journey.

We understand
the business We design
the strategy We build
the solution We keep
evolving

01 —

We understand
the business

Before designing a solution, we work out what actually needs to change.

We look at the organization's goals, processes, platforms and challenges to find where the opportunities for change are and which capabilities the business needs to build.

We analyze

  • Strategic objectives.
  • Critical processes.
  • Platforms and architecture.
  • Data and information sources.
  • The needs of the people and of the business.

The outcome

A clear picture of the challenge and of the opportunities worth pursuing.

02 —

We design
the strategy

We map the route before choosing the technology.

From that understanding of the business, we design a strategy that sets out the priorities, capabilities and solutions needed to reach the agreed goals.

Artificial intelligence is built in from the outset wherever it can add new capabilities, improve processes or change how decisions get made.

We design

  • Transformation strategies.
  • New processes and operating models.
  • Architectures and platforms.
  • Digital and AI capabilities.
  • Priorities and an evolution roadmap.

The outcome

A strategy aligned to the business, and a concrete route to deliver it.

03 —

We build
the solution

We turn the strategy into capabilities that work.

We design and build solutions that bring together technology, data and artificial intelligence, focused on the processes and needs already identified.

This is not about installing a tool: we build platforms and solutions ready to evolve as the needs of the business change.

We bring together

  • Technology and architecture.
  • Data and artificial intelligence.
  • Applications and platforms.
  • Process automation.
  • Integrations and APIs.

The outcome

Solutions in production, ready to grow alongside the business.

04 —

We keep
evolving

Transformation does not end when the solution goes live.

We measure results, fold in what we learn and adjust the solutions to meet new needs, new data and new opportunities.

Evolving continuously is what keeps platforms, processes and capabilities in step with a business that never stops changing.

We evolve

  • We measure results.
  • We look at new challenges.
  • We add new capabilities.
  • We refine processes and solutions.
  • We adapt platforms to new needs.

The outcome

A lasting ability to evolve.

Transformation is not a project with an end date.
It is an organization's ability to keep evolving.
